"Mini valet or full valet?" is the question we hear most. The short version: **a mini valet keeps a clean car clean; a full valet resets a car that needs it.** Book the wrong one and you either pay for work you did not need or end up disappointed with the result.

Here is exactly what each includes, how long it takes and how to choose.

## Mini valet vs full valet at a glance

| | Mini valet | Full valet |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Maintain a well-kept car | Deep clean and reset |
| Exterior | Snow foam and hand wash, wheels, dry | Everything in a mini valet + paint protection finish |
| Interior | Vacuum, dash and surface wipe-down, glass | Full vacuum, steam clean, upholstery shampoo, leather clean and condition |
| Time | 60–90 minutes | Up to 2 hours |
| Typical UK price | £35–£65 | £85–£145 |
| Ecoverde price | From £109 | From £189 (Deluxe Full Valet) |
| How often | Every 2–6 weeks | Every 3–6 months |

## What is included in a mini valet?

A mini valet is a thorough tidy-up for cars that are cleaned regularly:

- pH-neutral snow foam pre-wash and two-bucket hand wash
- Wheels and tyres cleaned
- Hand dry with microfibre
- Interior vacuum, including footwells
- Wipe-down of the dashboard, console and door cards
- Glass inside and out

It is ideal between full valets, before a weekend away, or if your car is kept garaged and only needs a refresh.

## What is included in a full valet?

A full valet is our deluxe deep clean. Everything in the mini valet, plus:

- **Steam sanitisation** of vents, seams, cup holders and door pockets
- **Upholstery shampoo** to lift stains from fabric seats and carpets
- **Leather clean and condition** to keep seats soft and crack-free
- Boot and seat rails vacuumed and cleaned
- Paint protection finish so the car stays cleaner for longer

If your car has pet hair, child seats, sand, food spills or a smell you cannot shift, it needs a full valet — or an [interior deep clean](/services/interior-deep-clean) if the inside is the main problem.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does a mini valet take?
Around 60–90 minutes for most cars. Larger vehicles or very dusty interiors can take a little longer.

### How long does a full valet take?
Our Deluxe Full Valet typically takes up to 2 hours. Cars with heavy soiling, pet hair or stains can take longer, which is why we quote from photos.

### Is a full valet worth it?
Yes, if the car has not been deep cleaned for a few months. Steam and shampoo remove bacteria, allergens and smells that a vacuum cannot reach, and the protection step keeps the paint cleaner for longer.

### Does a mini valet include the inside?
Yes — a vacuum, a wipe-down of the main surfaces and the glass inside. It does not include steam, shampoo or leather treatment.

### Can I upgrade from a mini to a full valet on the day?
Usually, yes. If we arrive and the car needs more than a mini valet, we will tell you before starting and only proceed once you are happy with the price.
