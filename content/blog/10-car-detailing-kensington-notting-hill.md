## Caring for supercars and classics

Supercars often have soft paint, complex aero and delicate finishes; classics may have single-stage paint and original chrome. Both need a careful, least-aggressive approach:

- Dedicated mitts, wheel brushes and pH-neutral products
- Inspection under detailing lights before any polishing
- Test spots to find the gentlest method that works
- Hand-applied protection suited to the paint type
- Careful leather and interior care — never harsh all-purpose cleaners

> **Tip:** for cars stored long-term or used only at weekends, a ceramic coating or quality sealant makes dust and pollen far easier to remove safely.

## Convertibles: soft-top cleaning and reproofing

Fabric roofs trap dirt, moss and algae, and lose their water repellency over time. Guidance from roof-care specialists is to deep clean at least twice a year — spring and autumn — and reproof after cleaning or at least once a year, especially for cars parked outside ([Classic Car Hub](https://classiccarhub.co.uk/classic-car-soft-top-care-guide/); [Ultramar](https://www.ultramarxl.com/en/blogs/blog/roadmap-cabriodak-cleaning-and-impregnating)). Pressure washers can damage seams, so roofs should always be cleaned by hand.
