## What is mobile car valeting?

Mobile car valeting means the valeter brings everything — water, power, products and equipment — to wherever your car is parked. A good mobile valet is not a quick wipe-over; it is the same deep clean you would get at a professional unit, done at your door.

At Ecoverde we work from an **electric van with no generator**, so there is no engine noise or fumes outside your home, and we use biodegradable, solvent-free products and low-water techniques throughout.

## What happens on the day

1. **Arrival.** We park nearby and set up — no hoses across the pavement, no need for your water or electricity.
2. **Pre-wash.** pH-neutral snow foam softens and lifts dirt before anything touches the paint.
3. **Hand wash.** Two-bucket method with soft mitts, then wheels, arches and tyres.
4. **Interior.** Full vacuum, steam on vents, seams and cup holders, and shampoo or leather care as booked.
5. **Finish.** Glass streak-free, protection applied, and a final walk-round with you.

A mini valet takes around 60–90 minutes; a deluxe full valet up to 2 hours.

## How to choose a mobile valeter in West London

There are plenty of options listed on marketplaces and directories, so a few checks go a long way:

- **Reviews in volume.** Look for a long track record across more than one platform. Ecoverde has 560+ verified reviews averaging 5.0 across Google, Checkatrade, Bark, Trustindex, Facebook and Yell.
- **Insurance.** Ask for public liability cover — we are fully insured.
- **Method.** Ask how they wash. If the answer does not include a pre-wash and two buckets, expect swirl marks.
- **Products.** Biodegradable, pH-balanced products are kinder to paint, trim and the drains on your street.
- **A real quote.** A valeter who wants photos before pricing is one who will not surprise you on the day.

> **Tip:** ask whether the valeter needs your water or power. If they do, check where the hose will run and that the generator will not disturb your neighbours.
