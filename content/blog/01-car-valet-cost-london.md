## What changes the price of a car valet?

Two cars booked for the "same" full valet can take very different amounts of time. These are the factors every honest valeter prices on:

- **Size of the vehicle.** A Fiat 500 and a seven-seat Range Rover are not the same job — larger cars typically cost 20–50% more.
- **Condition.** Pet hair, sand, mould, smoke smells or months without a clean can double the interior time.
- **Level of finish.** A tidy-up is quick; steam sanitising every vent and seam, shampooing seats and protecting the paint takes hours.
- **Mobile vs fixed site.** Mobile valeting saves you the drive and the wait, which is why it usually costs a little more than a drive-through.
- **Products and equipment.** pH-neutral snow foam, steam machines and professional coatings cost more than a bucket and sponge — and protect your paint far better.

## Mobile valet vs hand car wash: is it worth paying more?

A roadside hand car wash is fine for a quick rinse, but it is rarely gentle on paint. Shared mitts and brushes drag grit across the clear coat, which is where most swirl marks come from. A mobile valet is slower and more careful: snow foam lifts dirt before anything touches the paint, and the two-bucket method keeps grit off your car.

| | Hand car wash | Mobile valet |
|---|---|---|
| Comes to you | No | Yes — home or work |
| Time you spend | Drive + queue | None |
| Paint-safe method | Varies | Snow foam + two-bucket |
| Interior detail | Basic | Steam, shampoo, odour treatment |
| Eco impact | High water use | Low-water, biodegradable |
