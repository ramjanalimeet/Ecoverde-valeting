Look at your car in direct sunlight. If the paint is covered in fine circular scratches that look like a spider's web, those are **swirl marks** — and almost every car in London has them. The fix is **paint correction**: carefully machine polishing the clear coat until the defects are gone and the gloss comes back.

## What causes swirl marks?

Swirls are tiny scratches in the clear coat. The most common causes are:

- Automatic car washes with brushes
- Washing with a single bucket or a dirty sponge
- Drying with a rough towel or chamois
- Wiping dust off a dry car
- Dealership "valets" done in a hurry

Dark colours show them most. Black, dark blue and dark grey cars can look grey and hazy in the sun even when they are clean.

## Single-stage vs multi-stage paint correction

| | Enhancement / single-stage | Two-stage correction | Multi-stage correction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Removes | Light swirls, haze, dullness | Moderate swirls and marring | Heavy defects, deeper scratches |
| Process | One polish and pad | Cut, then refine | Several cutting and refining steps |
| Gloss improvement | Noticeable | Major | Maximum |
| Best for | Newer cars, light colours | Most daily drivers | Neglected or show cars |
| Time | Hours | A full day or more | Multiple days |

Dark colours show defects most, so they often need at least a two-stage correction to look right, while lighter colours can sometimes get away with a single stage. Read more about [the different levels of paint correction](https://www.detailedimage.com/Ask-a-Pro/different-levels-of-paint-correction).

## Our paint correction process

1. **Decontamination wash** — snow foam, two buckets, iron and tar removal, clay bar.
2. **Inspection** — panel by panel under detailing lights.
3. **Test spot** — we find the least aggressive pad and polish that removes the defects.
4. **Correction** — single or multi-stage machine polishing.
5. **Refine** — a finishing polish for maximum clarity.
6. **Protect** — wax, sealant or [ceramic coating](/services/ceramic-coating) to lock in the result.

## Can deep scratches be polished out?

If your fingernail catches in a scratch, it has probably gone through the clear coat. Polishing can make it far less visible, but a full fix needs a [scratch or bumper repair](/services/bumper-scuff-repair) or [bodywork respray](/services/bodywork-respray). We will always tell you honestly which is which.

## How to keep paint swirl-free after correction

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Pre-wash with snow foam | Use automatic brush car washes |
| Use two buckets and a clean mitt | Use sponges or brushes on paint |
| Dry with a plush microfibre or blower | Wipe dust off a dry car |
| Protect with sealant or ceramic | Leave bird lime to bake in the sun |

## Frequently asked questions

### Is paint correction permanent?
The defects removed are gone for good, but new swirls will appear if the car is washed badly. Safe washing and a protective coating keep it looking corrected for years.

### How long does paint correction take?
