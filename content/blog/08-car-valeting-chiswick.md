## Parking and access tips for a valet in W4

| Situation | What works best |
|---|---|
| Residents' parking bay | Just tell us the street — we park nearby and work on your car where it is |
| Underground or office car park | Check headroom and that the manager is happy; we need no power or water |
| Under trees | Ideal for a valet + protection — sap and bird lime come off easier on protected paint |
| Narrow street | We keep the pavement clear and work tidily around the car |

> **Tip:** cars parked under Chiswick's lime and plane trees pick up sticky sap and bird lime fast. A sealant or [ceramic coating](/services/ceramic-coating) makes it far easier to wipe off before it etches the paint.
