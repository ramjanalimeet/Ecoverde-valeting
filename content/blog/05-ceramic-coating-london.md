Ceramic coating is one of the most searched-for car care services in London, and one of the most over-hyped. Done properly, it gives your paint a hard, glossy, water-repellent layer that makes washing easier for years. Done badly, it locks swirl marks in under a shiny shell.

Here is what a **ceramic coating** really does, how long it lasts, what it costs and whether it is worth it for your car.

## What is a ceramic coating?

A ceramic coating is a liquid polymer (typically silica-based) that is applied by hand and cures into a thin, hard layer bonded to your car's clear coat. It is not a forcefield — it will not stop stone chips or deep scratches — but it does:

- Repel water, so dirt and grime slide off more easily (hydrophobic)
- Protect the paint from UV fading and oxidation
- Resist bird lime, tree sap and road salt better than wax
- Add depth and gloss to the paint
- Make every future wash quicker and safer

## How long does ceramic coating last?

Most professional coatings last **around 2–5 years**, with premium systems claiming longer. In reality, lifespan depends far more on preparation and aftercare than on the label. Automatic brush car washes shorten a coating's life dramatically ([Chemical Guys](https://www.chemicalguys.com/blogs/exterior-how-tos/how-long-does-ceramic-coating-last); [Autotrader](https://www.autotrader.com/car-shopping/ceramic-coating-lifespan)).

## Ceramic coating vs wax vs sealant

| | Carnauba wax | Synthetic sealant | Ceramic coating |
|---|---|---|---|
| Typical durability | Weeks to a few months | Several months | Years, with care |
| Gloss | Warm, deep | Sharp | Deep, glassy |
| Water repellency | Good when fresh | Good | Excellent |
| Chemical resistance | Low | Medium | High |
| Prep needed | Wash | Wash + decontaminate | Wash, decontaminate, polish |
| Cost | Lowest | Low–medium | Highest upfront |

## How much does ceramic coating cost in London?

There is no single price, because the coating itself is only part of the job. UK guides commonly quote a few hundred pounds for a standard car, with London at the higher end and larger vehicles costing more. The biggest variable is **paint correction**: if the paint has swirls, they must be polished out *before* coating, otherwise the coating seals them in.

| What affects the price | Why |

| Vehicle size | More panels, more product, more time |
| Paint condition | Swirls and scratches need machine polishing first |
| Coating product | Durability and layers vary |

## Is ceramic coating worth it?

It is worth it if you plan to keep the car for a few years, wash it yourself or with a mobile valeter, park outdoors, or simply want the paint to stay glossy with less effort. It is less worth it if the car is about to be sold or the paint is badly damaged and needs a respray first.

## How to make a ceramic coating last

- Wash every 2–3 weeks with a pH-neutral shampoo and two buckets
- Avoid automatic brush car washes
- Remove bird lime and sap quickly
- Dry with a clean microfibre towel or air blower
- Book a top-up or maintenance wash — our [maintenance packages](/services/maintenance-packages) are designed for coated cars

## Frequently asked questions

### Can ceramic coating be applied at home?
Yes — we apply ceramic coatings at your home or workplace. The car must stay dry while the coating cures, so we plan around the weather.

### Does ceramic coating remove scratches?
No. Swirls and light scratches must be removed by machine polishing first. The coating then protects the corrected finish.

### How soon can I wash a ceramic-coated car?
Coatings need time to cure before the first wash — the exact period depends on the product. We will give you clear aftercare instructions for the coating used on your car, then recommend washing with a pH-neutral shampoo.

### Is ceramic coating worth it on a new car?
Often, yes. New cars still pick up swirls in transit and at the dealer, so a light enhancement polish before coating gives the best result.
